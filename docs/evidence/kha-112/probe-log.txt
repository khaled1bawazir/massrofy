SEED DATA (real ingestion pipeline, not a DAO back door)
------------------------------------------------------------------
`adb emu sms send D360 ...` was ABANDONED: the emulator console strips the
alphabetic prefix off an alphanumeric sender, so "D360" is delivered as "360",
which no senderPattern in assets/rule_packs/sa-core.json can match. Verified:

  $ adb shell content query --uri content://sms/inbox --projection address:body
  Row: 0 address=360, body=D360: Outgoing transfer of SAR 2,000.00 ...

`adb shell content insert` also cannot be used — its --bind parser splits on
every ':' and every bank template contains a HH:mm timestamp:
  [ERROR] Binding not well formed: body:s:D360: Purchase of SAR 89.00 ...

Final method: adb root + sqlite3 straight into the telephony provider's CE
database (/data/user/0/com.android.providers.telephony/databases/mmssms.db),
which is the exact URI SmsChannel.kt's sweep reads
(Telephony.Sms.Inbox.CONTENT_URI, _ID > watermark). The app then ingested them
through its own real path — IngestWorker ran and returned SUCCESS per message.

Six messages, templates copied structurally from
test/fixtures/synthetic_sms_corpus.dart (d360-01 / d360-03 / d360-04):
  BALAD COFFEE ROASTERS   62.25 SAR  debit  affectsSpend
  SARA K ALHARBI       2,000.00 SAR  debit  affectsSpend   (transfer out)
  MERIDIAN LOGISTICS   9,750.00 SAR  credit NOT spend      (transfer in)
  BALAD COFFEE ROASTERS   45.50 SAR  debit  affectsSpend
  PANDA HYPERMARKET 118  310.40 SAR  debit  affectsSpend
  BALAD COFFEE ROASTERS   89.00 SAR  debit  affectsSpend
